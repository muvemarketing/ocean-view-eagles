Frequently asked

Curious about becoming an Eagle?

Common questions about membership, events, and what to expect during your visit to Ocean View Eagles Club #4528. You've come to the right place.

How can I become a member?

Joining our club is easy. Visit us at 35083 Atlantic Ave and our friendly staff will walk you through the membership process. You can also email Brandy at foemembership@gmail.com or call (302) 616-2937 for details on options and benefits.

Are non-members allowed to visit?

Absolutely. While we encourage membership for the full range of benefits, non-members are welcome to visit and enjoy our open events and dining areas. Members-only sections and events do have access restrictions; guests must sign in at the entrance with a sponsor present.

What types of events does the club host?

Live music nights, themed parties, game tournaments, and charitable fundraisers. Thursday is our Cheesesteak Day; Saturdays feature live entertainment plus the Eagles' Nest bar. Our annual April benefit supports Our Rescue and Zoë Ministries of Delaware.

Is there a dress code?

Casual and relaxed. Specific events may have their own guidelines which we'll share beforehand.

Is there parking?

Yes — ample parking for members and guests. An additional lot opens after 5 PM.

Who does the club support?

Seven partner organizations: Frankford Public Library, Our Rescue, South Coastal Village Volunteers, Code Purple Sussex (via Love INC of Mid-Delmarva), Diakonia, Folds of Honor, and Zoë Ministries of Delaware. See the Causes page for each partner's story.
