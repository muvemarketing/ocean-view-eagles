Giving back

Our commitment to charitable endeavors.

At Aerie #4528 we believe in the power of giving back to our community and making a positive impact on the lives of those in need. Seven partner organizations receive our support year-round.

7 partners Year-round Sussex · Worcester · Delaware
Seven partners

The organizations we stand with.

Click any partner below to visit their site and learn how they serve our shared community.

Frankford Public Library

A beacon of knowledge and learning in our community — providing access to information, education, and cultural resources for Sussex County neighbors.

Visit partner →

Our Rescue (O.U.R.)

Operation Underground Railroad — combatting human trafficking and supporting survivors through field operations, survivor care, and prevention.

Visit partner →

South Coastal Village Volunteers

Coordinates volunteers to assist members with services like transportation to appointments, home visits, telephone check-ins, errands, technology support, and small household tasks.

Visit partner →

Code Purple Sussex

Run by Love INC of Mid-Delmarva — helps those suffering from homelessness, abuse, financial struggles, and addiction with housing, resources, counseling, and guidance.

Visit partner →

Diakonia

Helping men, women, and families in Worcester County, MD by providing emergency housing and food assistance — filling a gap that keeps neighbors safe.

Visit partner →

Folds of Honor

Providing educational scholarships to the spouses and children of America's fallen and disabled service members. A cause close to the heart of every Eagle.

Visit partner →

Zoë Ministries of Delaware

A catalyst for change in Delaware since 2012 — supporting survivors of human trafficking through advocacy, safe housing, and long-term restoration.

Cause story · Our Rescue

Aerie's benefit to end human trafficking.

Our annual April benefit raises funds split between Our Rescue (Operation Underground Railroad) and Zoë Ministries of Delaware. DJ, auctions, 50/50s, and a quilt raffle — noon to 5 PM at the clubhouse.

Every dollar rolls straight to survivors and the work it takes to bring them home.

Cause story · Rallying together

When one of ours needs us.

"Rallying Together for Brian" was one of the benefits our Aerie has organized for a member of our own community in need. Eagles show up for Eagles.

When you see a donation jar on the bar, or a raffle quilt on the wall — every one of them is for a neighbor.
