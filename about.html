The Aerie

Fun, friends, and fellowship.

Welcome to Ocean View Eagles Club #4528 — a vibrant community hub where friendship, camaraderie, and shared experiences take center stage. Nestled in the heart of Ocean View, the Aerie is a home for folks seeking connection, laughter, and a welcoming atmosphere.

Our mission

Connect, unwind, and celebrate.

At Ocean View Eagles Club #4528, our mission goes beyond providing a venue — we're dedicated to creating a haven where members forge lasting friendships, unwind at the end of a long week, and actively participate in a community that cares.

Whether you're a lifelong Eagle or walking through the doors for the first time, you'll find the same welcome: a cold drink, a friendly face, and a seat worth settling into.

Things to do

Five ways to spend a Tuesday.

Monday fun

Flip Five

Easy to learn and even more fun to play — players match their cards with the dealer's. A game of skill and chance that keeps everyone on their toes.

Sing your heart out

Karaoke nights

Step into the spotlight and let your voice soar. Karaoke nights are music, laughter, and the thrill of performing — everyone becomes a star.

Diverse tunes

Live music

Acoustic sets to lively bands — our live music nights showcase local talent across genres, ensuring something for every musical palate.

Rack 'em up

Quality pool table

Feel the satisfaction of a well-executed shot or laugh off a friendly miss — our pool tables are perfect for casual play or friendly competition.

Bullseye

Two dart boards

For the competitive spirit or the laid-back tosser alike. Darts at Aerie #4528 is dynamic and engaging — and always good for a laugh.

The bar

Eagles' Nest

Open Saturdays for a cold drink and a sandwich. Come and be part of the laid-back atmosphere and cheerful banter that the Nest promises to deliver.

Worthy President · 2024–2025

Home Aerie of Eric B. Davis.

Eric B. Davis serves as Worthy President of Aerie #4528 and the PA/DE State Chaplain for the Fraternal Order of Eagles for the 2024–2025 term.

With a deep commitment to service, community, and fellowship, Eric has been an integral part of the Eagles family for many years. As Worthy President, he leads with passion and dedication — overseeing the initiatives that strengthen the Eagles' mission to support charitable causes and enhance the well-being of local communities.
