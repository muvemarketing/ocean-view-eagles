Our history

A century-old order, a newer Aerie.

Aerie #4528 has been a home for fellowship and service on the Sussex County coast since our 2009 charter — and our current clubhouse opened at 35083 Atlantic Ave in Summer 2013.

Early years

In the early years of our Aerie, we laid the foundation for what would become a thriving community organization — a place where members could gather for fellowship, service, and celebration in Sussex County.

2013New clubhouse

New location · 35083 Atlantic Ave

In the summer of 2013, Ocean View Eagles Club #4528 embarked on a new chapter with the opening of our new location at 35083 Atlantic Ave, Ocean View, DE — the clubhouse that remains our home today.

2024Eagles' Nest opens

The Eagles' Nest

We opened the doors to The Eagles' Nest — an addition to the clubhouse for members looking for a laid-back seat, a cold drink, and a cheerful regular crowd. Stop in Saturdays for a sandwich.

TodayWorthy President Eric B. Davis

Expanding reach, growing impact

With our clubhouse on Atlantic Ave, Aerie #4528 has expanded reach and impact within the Sussex County community. From Aerie #4528 we've run fundraisers for Our Rescue, Zoë Ministries of Delaware, Folds of Honor, Frankford Public Library, South Coastal Village, Code Purple Sussex, and Diakonia.

Looking aheadYear after year

The next chapter

As Aerie #4528 looks to the future, we remain steadfast in our dedication to the Order's core principles: community service, fellowship, and camaraderie. The promise stays simple — people helping people.
